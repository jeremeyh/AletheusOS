# ADR-GP-00029 — Concept Collision Engine

Status: Proposed  
Classification: ADDITIVE  
Authority: Watch Tower™  

## Decision

Add a dedicated Concept Collision Engine to detect semantic duplication, authority overlap, and conceptual drift before new concepts enter the Super-Mesh.

## Rationale

AletheusOS has reached enough architectural breadth that conceptual duplication is a primary risk. The platform requires executable protection against redundant evolution.

## Consequences

Genesis, Watch Tower, Repository DNA, Atlas, and Council can eventually consume collision reports before accepting new architecture.
