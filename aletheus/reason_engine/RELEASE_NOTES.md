# Release Summary

Genesis 49.0 introduces the Reason Engine™, the constitutional reasoning capability of AletheusOS.

This release transforms the Foundation from one that can preserve governed experience into one that can derive governed conclusions.

The Reason Engine performs constitutional reasoning by separating inference, justification, and evaluation into distinct cognitive faculties.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Reason Engine exists to transform observations, evidence, memory, and constitutional principles into explainable constitutional conclusions.

The Reason Engine does not determine truth.

It determines which conclusions are currently justified.
