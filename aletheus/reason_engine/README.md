# Reason Engine™

**Genesis:** 49.0

**Version:** 1.0.0 RC1

---

# Purpose

The Reason Engine™ is the constitutional reasoning capability of AletheusOS.

It transforms observations, evidence, memory, and constitutional principles into explainable conclusions.

The Reason Engine does not determine truth.

It determines which conclusions are currently justified.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

Reason exists to explain.

Inference proposes possibilities.

Reason evaluates those possibilities.

Justification explains them.

Evaluation governs them.

Together they produce constitutional conclusions.

# Responsibilities

The Reason Engine is responsible for:

- Constitutional inference
- Constitutional justification
- Constitutional evaluation
- Reason chain preservation
- Confidence estimation
- Alternative generation

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Reason Engine intentionally does NOT perform:

- Identity management
- Memory persistence
- Truth determination
- Council governance
- Marketplace analysis
- Authentication
- Authorization
- Application-specific business logic

Those responsibilities belong to other Foundation capabilities.

---

# Reason Transparency Principle

Every constitutional conclusion shall preserve the reasoning that produced it.

No constitutional conclusion shall exist without an inspectable chain of justification.
