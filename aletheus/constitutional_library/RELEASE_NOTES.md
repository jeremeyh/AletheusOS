# Release Summary

Genesis 51.0 introduces the Constitutional Library™, the institutional knowledge capability of AletheusOS.

This release transforms the Foundation from one that can remember, reason, and explain into one that can preserve governed institutional knowledge.

Knowledge is now treated as a first-class constitutional artifact.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Constitutional Library exists to preserve institutional knowledge produced by constitutional cognition.

Knowledge is governed.

Knowledge is explainable.

Knowledge evolves.

Knowledge is never divorced from its constitutional provenance.
