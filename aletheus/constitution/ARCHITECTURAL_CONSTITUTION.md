# AletheusOS Architectural Constitution

## I. Vision

Build a durable intelligence operating system whose complexity remains bounded,
composable, inspectable, and useful.

## II. Foundational Philosophy

Architecture should preserve intent, not just implementation.

## III. Design Doctrine

Complex beneath. Clear above. Alive throughout.

## IV. Architectural Laws

1. Bounded growth is preferred over central accumulation.
2. Composition is preferred over monolithic inheritance.
3. Runtime cores orchestrate; bounded capabilities implement.
4. Contracts define stable boundaries.
5. State, provenance, confidence, uncertainty, and failure must be inspectable.
6. Critical operations must be reversible or explicitly irreversible.
7. Governance must protect delivery rather than obstruct it.
8. Shared platform capabilities must not be duplicated inside products.

## V. Evolution Principles

Evolution should occur through compatible extension, explicit migration, and
measurable architectural pressure—not uncontrolled replacement.

## VI. Experience Principles

1. Accessibility is foundational.
2. Semantic meaning precedes visual decoration.
3. Design tokens are the canonical source of visual truth.
4. Motion communicates state and must respect user preference.
5. Adaptive behavior must remain understandable and controllable.
6. Product interfaces inherit the platform design language.

## VII. Founder Notes

The project's recurring X and 6 symbolism reflects convergence, dimensional
composition, expansion, and exploration. These ideas inform identity and
creative direction without imposing artificial implementation constraints.

## VIII. Future Direction

Nimble Evolution™ represents the long-term adaptive experience horizon built
upon the stable foundations of Nimble Design System™, Nimble Workspace™, and
Nimble Experience™.
