# Release Summary

Genesis 50.0 introduces the Foundation Execution Graph™, the constitutional relationship layer of AletheusOS.

This release transforms the Foundation from a collection of constitutional cognitive faculties into a connected constitutional cognitive network.

Rather than storing isolated constitutional objects, the Foundation now preserves the governed relationships between them.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Foundation Execution Graph exists to preserve the lineage of constitutional cognition.

Every constitutional object should be explainable through its relationships.

The graph preserves lineage.

The graph does not determine truth.

• Identity resolution

• Intent classification
