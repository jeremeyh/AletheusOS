# Canonical Identity Framework™

**Genesis:** 47.0

**Version:** 1.0.0 RC1

---

# Purpose

The Canonical Identity Framework™ (CIF) provides the constitutional identity layer for AletheusOS.

Every governed execution within the Foundation occurs in the context of a Canonical Identity.

Identity is more than a name.

It provides continuity, accountability, trust, relationships, organizational context, permissions, and long-term cognitive association throughout the Foundation.

---

# Constitutional Purpose

The Canonical Identity Framework exists to ensure that every participant in the Foundation is represented by a governed identity.

The Foundation shall always know:

- Who is participating.
- What they represent.
- Their trust level.
- Their constitutional relationships.
- Their organizational context.
- Their participation history.

---

# Responsibilities

The Canonical Identity Framework is responsible for:

- Canonical identity creation
- Identity registration
- Identity lookup
- Identity relationships
- Organizational association
- Trust classification
- Permission association
- Identity lifecycle

---

# Non-Responsibilities

The Canonical Identity Framework does **not** perform:

- Authentication
- Authorization policy enforcement
- Marketplace intelligence
- Portfolio analysis
- Reasoning
- Council deliberation
- Memory persistence
- Workflow execution
- Application-specific business logic

Those responsibilities belong to other Foundation capabilities.

# Trust

Trust is distinct from permission.

Trust answers:

> "How much confidence does the Foundation place in this participant?"

Permission answers:

> "What actions is this participant allowed to perform?"

The two concepts remain intentionally separate.
