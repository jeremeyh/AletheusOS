# Nimble™ Visual Grammar

## Doctrine

> Complex beneath. Clear above. Alive throughout.

The Nimble™ visual system expresses intelligence through clarity,
depth, responsiveness, movement, and spatial continuity.

## Shape

Primary surfaces use soft geometry rather than rigid rectangular
containers.

Shape categories:

- compact controls use restrained radii,
- operational cards use medium radii,
- workspaces use large radii,
- environmental graphics may use amorphous geometry,
- pills are reserved for compact status and filtering,
- critical data tables remain structurally precise.

Amorphous shape must not reduce legibility or control predictability.

## Color

Gold represents canonical AletheusOS identity, authority, and resolved
value.

Violet represents intelligence, reasoning, context, and discovery.

Cyan represents activity, connectivity, energy, streaming, and live
system behavior.

Green, amber, and red communicate success, warning, and danger.

Color must never be the sole carrier of meaning.

## Depth

Depth communicates hierarchy and spatial relationship.

- flat surfaces represent base information,
- raised surfaces represent actionable or contextual information,
- floating surfaces represent temporary interaction,
- deep overlays represent focused or system-level activity.

Glass and blur may create atmosphere, but never at the cost of
contrast, readability, or performance.

## Typography

Typography must remain highly legible even when surrounding graphics
are expressive.

Display typography is used sparingly for identity, high-level status,
and major navigation transitions.

Operational data favors compact, stable typography.

Monospaced typography is reserved for identifiers, system state,
commands, logs, and technical provenance.

## Motion

Motion communicates causality and continuity.

Micro-motion confirms interaction.

Structural motion reveals navigation, resizing, docking, and hierarchy.

System motion communicates live activity and state.

Narrative motion explains reasoning, provenance, workflows, and change.

Ambient motion creates vitality without competing with content.

## Principle X

Every surface must expose enough information for the user to understand:

- current state,
- active operation,
- confidence,
- uncertainty,
- provenance,
- proposed consequence,
- failure cause,
- and reversibility.

Visual beauty must never conceal truth.
