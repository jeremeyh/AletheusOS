# Genesis 37 — Constitutional Experience Intelligence

## North Star
> AletheusOS should not feel like software running on a computer. It should feel like entering a living intelligence environment that continuously reorganizes itself around truth, evidence, and the user's mission.

Genesis 37 establishes CEIE, Information Physics, AxiomUX™, Topological Fluidity, the separate Workspace Composer™, bounded Standard/Admin/Developer experiences, and the constitutionally isolated Founder Observatory.

The experience is liquid, amorphous, breathing, immersive, interactive, alive, responsive, and consciously aware in the experiential sense: continuously perceiving context, adapting, explaining, and self-organizing. It does not claim biological consciousness.

## Constitutional Law
Every visual behavior must be an explainable projection of evidence, confidence, constitutional policy, user intent, system state, or ergonomic support. Nothing moves merely for decoration.

## Founder Observatory
The Founder Observatory is the eye in the sky. It alone synthesizes ecosystem-total visibility: user marketplace activity, admin actions, developer contributions, builds, patches, releases, platform health, mission flow, architecture, risk, and strategic evolution.

It is not an admin role, not a developer feature, and not a Workspace Composer surface. It requires separate identity, authorization, signing, deployment, trust-root, telemetry, and audit boundaries. It exposes no public SDK, plugin hook, route registration, service discovery, or extension tunnel.

## Workspace Composer
Workspace Composer is a separate user-facing capability for easily building and customizing dashboards, instruments, layouts, mission workspaces, overlays, saved views, and templates without access to Founder internals.

## Information Physics
Informational mass, semantic gravity, evidence density, veracity phase, contradiction shear, temporal momentum, cognitive load, and consensus pressure govern visual state. Crystalline appearance means a declared verification policy was satisfied—not metaphysical infallibility.
