# AletheusOS Constitution

## Preamble

AletheusOS exists to provide a constitutional foundation for intelligent software systems.

The Platform shall remain compositional, bounded, testable, governable, observable, and evolvable.

Intelligence belongs to capabilities.

Products belong to applications.

Governance belongs to the Constitution.

The Platform shall enable intelligence, but it shall not become the intelligence.

---

## Article I — Platform Purpose

The Platform exists to coordinate, host, govern, and protect intelligent systems.

The Platform shall not contain domain-specific business intelligence.

---

## Article II — Bounded Growth

Every component shall have one clearly bounded responsibility.

When responsibilities expand, components shall divide rather than accumulate.

---

## Article III — Runtime Protection

The Runtime executes.

The Runtime shall not own business logic, domain intelligence, product logic, or governance policy.

---

## Article IV — Executive Neutrality

The Executive governs.

The Executive shall evaluate, coordinate, and authorize, but it shall not compute domain algorithms.

---

## Article V — Platform Neutrality

The Platform coordinates subsystems.

The Platform shall remain reusable across applications and shall not specialize for one product.

---

## Article VI — Capability Ownership

Capabilities own reusable domain intelligence.

THORᵡ, Hawk A•eye™, Marketplace Intelligence, Asset Vault, Portfolio Engine, SPA, and Council are capabilities.

---

## Article VII — Application Composition

Applications compose capabilities into user-facing products.

Card Hawk is an application.

Card Hawk is not a capability.

---

## Article VIII — Adapter Rule

Adapters translate interfaces.

Adapters shall not become implementations.

---

## Article IX — Registry Rule

Registries describe.

Registries shall not execute.

---

## Article X — Proven Abstraction

No abstraction shall enter the Platform until at least two independent capabilities require it, it remains domain-neutral, and it reduces total system complexity.

---

## Article XI — Evolution Without Drift

Evolution is required.

Architectural drift is prohibited.

Every Genesis release shall strengthen clarity, composability, testability, and constitutional alignment.

---

## Article XII — SPA Stewardship

Spectrum Platform Analyzer shall serve as the constitutional auditor of AletheusOS.

SPA shall measure whether implementation continues to reflect this Constitution.
