# Archive, Museum, and Provenance Language

Genesis 27.5.0 establishes Archive, Museum, and Provenance Language as part of the Card Hawk
Experience Vocabulary Library.

## Experience Vocabulary Principle

Every AletheusOS application communicates through a domain-native
experiential vocabulary that is intuitive to its users while remaining
semantically faithful to the underlying constitutional platform
capabilities.

Platform Language defines how intelligence works.
Application Language defines what intelligence accomplishes.
Experience Vocabulary defines how humans naturally understand it.

Card Hawk is not limited to sports. Its language must serve trading cards,
TCGs, comics, coins, currency, watches, sneakers, fine art, memorabilia,
movie props, music memorabilia, autographs, toys, luxury collectibles,
historical artifacts, digital collectibles, and future asset classes.
