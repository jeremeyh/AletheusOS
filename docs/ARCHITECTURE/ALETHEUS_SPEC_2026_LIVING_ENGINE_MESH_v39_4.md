## Principles

- Engines are bounded services, not effects embedded in components.
- React remains composition and control.
- Deterministic runtime paths remain replayable and testable.
- Hardware acceleration is adaptive, not mandatory.
- SharedArrayBuffer is only used under cross-origin isolation.
- SDF optics and advanced physics must degrade gracefully.
- Predictive intent must remain local, transparent, bounded, and non-invasive.
- No engine may bypass constitutional authority, provenance, observability, or
  lifecycle governance.

## Future integration

The current SDF, layout, and constraint interfaces are canonical boundaries.
Future implementations may bind WebGPU, Taffy/Yoga, Rapier, Skia, or WASM
without changing AxiomUX or application contracts.
